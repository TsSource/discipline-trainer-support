Keep a habit. Raise a creature.

Need a hand? Write to sharma@sharmaautomation.com — say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened.

How does it work?

Every habit you track lays an egg. Check in once a day: when you've kept your promise, your creature eats its fish and grows — the first time, the egg hatches. It grows from baby to child, teen, adult and, at last, its prime.

I missed a day. What happens?

It mopes. After two bad days it packs its bag, and after three in a row it leaves in the night and leaves you a note. It never dies: three good days in a row bring it home, stronger than it left.

I slipped. Should I say so?

Yes. An honest slip costs less than silence: it mopes for a while, then carries on.

When does its diary get a page?

The day after. Each page is about a day that's over, so a creature that hatched today writes its first page tomorrow. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, the words come from Apple's on-device model; on other iPhones, from the app's own words.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit ▸ Add Widget, and choose Discipline Trainer, small or medium. To pick which creature it shows, touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

In the app: Settings ▸ Reminders. If they're on but nothing arrives, check iOS Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Discipline Trainer.

What does the full version unlock?

The free version keeps one habit. The full version — one purchase, no subscription — keeps as many as you like, each with its own creature.

I bought the full version. How do I get it back?

On any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account: Settings ▸ Full Version ▸ Restore Purchase.

Will my creatures come to a new iPhone?

Your habits and creatures live only on your iPhone — there's no account or server. They move to a new iPhone with everything else when you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I delete everything?

In the app: Settings ▸ Your Data ▸ Delete All Data. Deleting the app does the same.
